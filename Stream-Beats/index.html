— WHAT'S INSIDE

Built for music people.

Everything you actually need, nothing you don't. Clean, fast, and free forever.

Ad-Free

Zero ads, zero interruptions. Just your music, playing the way you want it.

Rust .bex Plugin System

Secure, auto-updating plugins for unlimited music sources. Memory-safe, fast, sandboxed.

Local + Online Unified

Your offline library alongside streams from YouTube Music, JioSaavn, Spotify & more.

Full-Screen Karaoke Lyrics

Time-synced karaoke view with manual offset. Works offline across all sources.

10-Band EQ + Crossfade

Per-profile equalizer and configurable crossfade (default 2s) on all platforms.

Last.fm & Discord

Scrobble with offline caching. Show what you're playing live on Discord.

Smart Replace

Auto-recovery finds a working stream if a track dies mid-playback. No skips.

Bulk Downloads

Download entire playlists or albums at once with the rebuilt download manager.

Backup & Restore

Export as JSON or M3U. Import on any device. Zero lock-in, your data is yours.

Global Charts

Daily-updated charts from plugins. Billboard, trending, top 50 — always fresh.

6 Languages

English, Hindi, Korean, German, Spanish, Japanese — all fully maintained.

AI Recommendations

Smarter suggestions based on your listening history via Last.fm and plugins.
